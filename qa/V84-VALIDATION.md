# v8.4.0 validation

The release adds three optional safe districts in Vrijhaven, eight one-time discoveries, Milo’s two-find quest, Linde’s rune workshop and two fixed crafting recipes. Main-page status text and footer layout were corrected.

- `npm test`: 295 rule and entrypoint-menu checks passed. The ten new exploration groups cover area locks, ordinary eight-direction keyboard routes to every gate/discovery/NPC, F interaction, full inventory and consumable pouches, one-time rewards, payment and level limits, elemental damage reduction, rune replacement, fixed item stats and save/checkpoint persistence.
- The actual `src/main.js` entrypoint is exercised in a minimal DOM. Its 18 menu groups include accepting and completing Milo’s quest, reading discoveries, opening Linde’s menu with F, selecting slots, paying for a rune, retaining scroll position, crafting a recipe without changing current equipment. Safe-area HUD checks hide the ordinary shop shortcut and show exploration text.
- Three full 24-chapter campaign simulations and five endgame trial combinations completed with ordinary earned gear and paid upgrades. The simulation grants no equipment, health, skill points or forced kills. These existing campaign bots do not acquire the new quest rewards or runes; navigation, purchases and elemental effects are verified by the dedicated new tests.
- Native Canvas decoded the three new WebP backgrounds and production sprites, then rendered Vrijhaven and all three new districts. Walkable floor overlays were checked against the artwork. The apartment cross-route was moved off furniture and new entry positions keep the hero clear of the return-gate labels. New background files are checked for a nonempty payload, as well as all item art references.
- All three new areas are safe. Returns do not refill health, mana or consumables. Discovery payments and crafted recipes are one-time across revisits, saves and checkpoint retry. Required finds occupy the quest log, not inventory. No new XP or legendary rewards are granted.
- Rune ranks require levels 5/10/16 and cost 280/800/1800 scrap. Their resistance contribution is 6/10/14%, with one secondary bonus per rank. Changing type removes the exact prior bonus before adding the replacement, charges 150 extra and preserves ordinary item improvements and current health/mana proportions. One rune fits on each equipped suit/head item.
- The cover removes the old V7 loading message after assets load. The help button has explicit spacing and the version footer uses normal flow, allowing scrolling on short screens. Browser CSS layout, browser frame rate, touch interaction and speaker output have not been manually tested; native rendering and DOM tests do not measure those.

Run `npm test` and `npm run site:build` with Node 18 or newer. GitHub Pages serves the repository root; Render can use the included static-site blueprint. Original generated-art prompts are in `assets/V84-ART-PROMPTS.json`.
